## Treatment

**A state broadcast engraved in gold.** The Federation's propaganda reel. It looks like the nation's own
banknotes, medals and recruitment posters have come alive. The visual language is guilloche rosettes (the
engraved spirals on currency) that bloom on every kick, sunburst rays, heraldry (a crowned eight-point star
with laurels and crossed sabers), art-deco architecture and silhouetted soldiers against huge suns. The fleet,
the medal and the crown are flat-shaded 3D with gold engraved edges, so 2D poster art and 3D share one look.

Palette: night navy `#0A1630` / `#13254B`, imperial gold `#D9AE52` (highlight `#F6DE9A`), parchment
`#F1E6CF`, and one accent, Federation crimson `#B0182A`. Type: **Cinzel** for royal inscriptions and
**Oswald** for military slogans. All slogans are original. No lyrics appear on screen, and every emblem and
figure is an original design. The crest, names and motto are placeholders you can swap.

Arc: **I. The Call** (bridge: the crest is forged, the sovereign, the army and the fleet are presented) →
**II. The Silence** (the dropout becomes a memorial; the band-out bar is the crown rising in darkness) →
**III. The Coronation** (final-chorus impact; balcony, crowds, the ascent on the belted peak) →
**IV. The Eternal Federation** (enlistment posters, the armada, the sovereign's portrait on the held note,
insignia montage, the wax seal on the last hit, the end card).
