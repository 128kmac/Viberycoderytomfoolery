## Treatment

**A Federation recruitment broadcast.** The Domini are a centuries-old crusader order for hire that hunts
scammers, beamers and exploiters. The film sells the order the way the order sees itself: the faceless hood
that terrifies, the ladder anyone can climb, the three hereditary houses that only birth can reach, and the
lord above them all, Emperyan, kind to the point of being terrifying. It never shows the massacre, but it
remembers the Azurelight's empty seat.

**Look.** Night and gold. Banknote guilloche and heraldry (the order is old, rich and proud) meet faceless
hooded silhouettes whose hood interior is pure shadow, with winged shoulder rings. House colours carry the
royal half: **Frigidus** frost blue, **Infernus** ember red, **Empyerus** white-gold sun. Fonts: Cinzel for
inscriptions and Oswald for orders and slogans.

**The hood is the motif.** The film opens by pushing into the shadow of a hood. The hood's void is used as a
wipe, and the climactic held note ends on Emperyan with his hood up: nothing inside but a small glowing smile.
